# 67Router Design System: Monolithic Architectural Luxury

Source Project: Sharp Minimalist Proxy Dashboard (Stitch ID: 290567993923632053)

## Core Philosophy & Aesthetic
- **Zero Softness:** Completely sharp geometry (`border-radius: 0px`) implemented universally without exceptions. No rounded pills, no soft corners.
- **Tonal Subtlety:** Deep obsidian and onyx (`#080808`, `#0F0F10`, `#141416`), modulated strictly through razor-thin 1px structural hairline armatures (`#222226`, `#2E2E33`).
- **Warm Metallic Precision:** Restrained brushed bronze (`#C5A880`) and champagne gold (`#E5C378`) accents deployed selectively for high-value interactions, telemetry, and key focal points.
- **Editorial Monoliths:** Grand typographic scale paired with hyper-legible, wide-tracked technical metadata.

## Color Tokens
### Surfaces
- `canvas`: `#080808` (Foundational background void with subtle 40px hairline grid)
- `surface-1`: `#0F0F10` (Primary paneling, cards, modular containers)
- `surface-2`: `#141416` (Raised interactive plates, headers, modal surfaces)
- `surface-hover`: `#1C1C1F` (Hover/active highlight states)

### Hairlines & Separators
- `border-base`: `#222226` (1px structural grid lines, dividing walls)
- `border-highlight`: `#2E2E33` (Active component frames, input outlines)

### Metallic Accents
- `bronze`: `#C5A880` (Primary brand accent, active indicator, focus borders)
- `gold`: `#E5C378` (Elevated CTA surfaces, highest-priority badges)
- `bronze-muted`: `#8C7656` (Disabled states, subtle labels)

### Typography
- `text-primary`: `#F5F5F7` (Headers, titles, primary readouts)
- `text-secondary`: `#A1A1A6` (Body text, secondary descriptions)
- `text-tertiary`: `#68686E` (Micro-labels, timestamps, serial stamps)

## Fonts
- **Universal Font:** `Momo Trust Sans` (Geometric Sans-Serif, weights 300 to 800) applied across all headlines, body text, buttons, navigation, and telemetry metadata.
